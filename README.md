## 🎬 1. Cinematic Camera Movement Formulas

When generating videos on [Gemini Omni](https://gemini-omni.dev/), specifying precise camera motion prevents hallucinated morphing and generates cinema-quality results.

### 📐 Master Formula
```
[Shot Scale] + [Subject & Action] + [Camera Motion] + [Lighting & Ambiance] + [Lens / Film Stock]
```

### 🎥 Movement Dictionary
| Movement Tag | Description | Example Prompt |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[Drone Forward Push]` | Slow, steady aerial dolly-in toward the scene | `Drone forward push over a misty Scandinavian fjord at sunrise, 35mm anamorphic lens, hyper-realistic, photorealistic reflections --ar 16:9` |
| `[Orbit 360]` | Smooth continuous circular camera rotation around the subject | `Orbit 360 around a futuristic cyberpunk cyber-samurai standing on a rainy neon-lit Tokyo rooftop, volumetric fog, dramatic rim light` |
| `[Whip Pan]` | Rapid directional transition connecting two focal elements | `Whip pan from a roaring roaring race car engine to the driver's determined eyes, motion blur, cinema verite style` |
| `[Slow Pullback]` | Backward tracking shot revealing environmental scale | `Slow pullback from an astronaut's visor reflecting a collapsing supernova, infinite cosmic dust, cinematic 4K master` |

## ⏱️ 2. Start & End Keyframe Directing Recipes

In [Gemini Omni Studio](https://gemini-omni.dev/studio), you can lock the **Start Frame** and **End Frame** to control temporal trajectories without jitter:

### Scene A: Cyberpunk Metamorphosis
- **Start Keyframe**: `A vintage 1980s retro desktop computer sitting in a dim dusty garage.`
- **End Keyframe**: `The computer morphs into a glowing quantum crystal server humming with blue photon rings.`
- **Directing Prompt**: `Smooth temporal morph, organic cybernetic circuits spreading through the chassis, volumetric bioluminescent lighting.`

### Scene B: Weather & Time Transition
- **Start Keyframe**: `A cozy alpine cabin covered in snow during an overcast winter twilight.`
- **End Keyframe**: `The same alpine cabin in vibrant spring sunshine surrounded by blooming wildflowers.`
- **Directing Prompt**: `Accelerated time-lapse transition, snow melting into crystal clear streams, gradual warm sunbeams breaking through retreating clouds.`

## 🎨 3. Multi-Scene Character Consistency Formula

To maintain consistent faces and costumes across multiple clips in [Gemini Omni](https://gemini-omni.dev/):

1. **Define Anchor Traits**: Use specific tags (e.g. `wearing a weathered brown aviator jacket, scar across left eyebrow, messy charcoal hair`).
2. **Reuse Image-to-Video Seed**: Generate character concept art in [Image-to-Image](https://gemini-omni.dev/studio/image-to-image), then feed into [Image-to-Video](https://gemini-omni.dev/studio/image-to-video).
